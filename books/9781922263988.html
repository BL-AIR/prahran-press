The Art of Surrender

by Blair Purvis

Ayman Saliba arrives at a Moroccan hammām uncertain of everything. Lucas Pfifer is already there — wealthy, Swiss, composed, and quietly falling apart.

What begins in the steam and silence of El Jadida unfolds across the Mediterranean, the Alps, and finally Sydney. Lucas privately seeks surrender as a form of escape. Ayman, newly out and still learning himself, must come to understand that care is not the same as submission. When Lucas misapplies his past patterns to their relationship — culminating in betrayal, self-destruction, and confrontation — both men are forced to redefine what surrender truly means.

Not a story about discovery. A story about what it costs to allow yourself to be truly known.
