Author

Blair Purvis

Novelist — Literary Fiction

Blair is a Melbourne-based author of literary fiction whose work lives at the intersection of desire, geography, and the slow, difficult business of being known. Drawn to stories that cross borders — emotional as much as physical — Blair writes characters who travel far in search of the things they are most afraid to find in themselves.

A passionate traveller and proud gay dad, Blair brings to the page a deep curiosity about the lives men live privately versus the lives they perform publicly, and the particular tenderness and damage that accumulates in the space between the two.

His debut novel, The Art of Surrender, is set across Morocco, Gibraltar, Zürich, St Moritz, Berlin, and Sydney. It follows Ayman Saliba and Lucas Pfifer — two men whose lives collide in the steam and silence of a Moroccan hammām and whose relationship unfolds over years, continents, crises, and a reckoning that arrives just in time, or possibly too late. Told in alternating points of view, the novel is about the slow, reluctant, and sometimes devastating process by which two people learn to let themselves be known — and what it costs to resist that surrender for too long.

Blair lives in Melbourne with his family.
