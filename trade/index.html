Terms in brief

  • Ordering on invoice needs a signed trade agreement. New to us? Just place your order with your shop's details; we'll email the agreement for you to sign, then send your invoice.
  • Nothing is charged when you submit. We check every order and email your invoice, usually within one business day.
  • Pay by card from the invoice, or by bank transfer quoting the invoice number.
  • Delivery is free within metropolitan Melbourne, and free elsewhere on 5 or more copies.
  • Every Prahran Publishing title is available on these terms. Editions marked “direct order only” cannot be taken on consignment.
  • Prefer to pay by card now? Order from our online shop instead.
